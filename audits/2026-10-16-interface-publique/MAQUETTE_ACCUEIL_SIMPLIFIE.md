# Maquette de l'accueil mobile simplifié

Aperçu proposé le 16 octobre 2026 ; aucune modification de l'application pour cette maquette. Les produits, prix et photos des boutiques sont illustratifs.

- Image : `maquette-accueil-mobile-simplifie-v1.png`.
- Mode : outil imagegen intégré, génération d'une maquette à partir du logo et des photos de catégories existants.
- Structure : recherche, six catégories sur deux rangées de trois cartes, quatre offres dans « À découvrir », quatre boutiques et menu Accueil / Catalogue / Panier / Compte.
- Bannière mobile, titre de recherche répété et bouton général retirés de la proposition.

## Prompt final

Use case: ui-mockup.
Asset type: one polished mobile homepage concept for the existing BabaMarket marketplace. This is a preview of a simpler proposed interface, not a deployed page.
Input images: Image 1 is the actual Baba Market logo and must be faithfully preserved as a small brand mark in the header. Image 2 is a reference atlas for category photography, not an edit target; use its food, villa, grocery shop, parcel and sale-tag photos as visual references. Image 3 is the services-category photograph: faithfully reuse its THREE BLACK professionals representing cleaning, repair and office/IT work. Do NOT use the barber-scissors photo from the atlas.
Create a single crisp, realistic full-page mobile UI screenshot, portrait approximately 1080x2400, edge-to-edge page only, no device frame, no perspective, no presentation board, no captions outside the UI. Design at a realistic 390px mobile CSS width, scaled sharply. Match the existing identity: warm ivory #faf9f6 background, white cards, emerald #087f5b, dark near-black text, simple readable sans-serif typography, moderate 12px corner radii, subtle borders, generous but efficient spacing.
Show these elements in precisely this order:
1. Compact white header, original small logo beside the wordmark "BabaMarket" at left, shopping-bag icon with small badge "4" at right. Below it a small location line with pin icon "Rabat, Maroc". Then a single pale rounded search field with magnifying glass and placeholder "Rechercher un produit, un service…".
2. DIRECTLY below the search, exactly SIX equally sized photo category cards in TWO ROWS OF THREE, always 3 across, with small dark labels and subtle right arrows. First row: "Produits et repas", "Services", "Locations". Second row: "Boutiques", "Livraison de colis", "Promotions". Food photo, Black multitrade professionals photo, villa photo, grocer shopfront, parcel, green sale tag respectively. Services photo must clearly show Black professionals as in image 3. No heading above the category grid.
3. One section heading "À découvrir", with NO adjacent "Tout voir" link. Exactly FOUR offer cards arranged TWO ACROSS in two rows, realistic photos, title and emerald price, no lengthy descriptions. Card 1 "Poisson braisé" / "80 DH", appetizing grilled fish with lemon. Card 2 "Attiéké frais" / "45 DH", a bowl of attieke and fish. Card 3 "Beurre de karité" / "35 DH", an actual jar of shea butter. Card 4 "Coiffure & tresses" / "120 DH", a Black female hairdresser braiding a Black woman's hair. Three product cards have small emerald add (+) buttons; the service card has a small outlined calendar booking icon. Photos must accurately match their offer, never computer hackers or movie posters.
4. One section heading "Boutiques", with NO adjacent "Tout voir" link. Exactly FOUR compact shop cards arranged TWO ACROSS in two rows, each with shop photography above and one short name plus subtle arrow below: "Moctar Express", "Saveurs & Terroir", "Épicerie Exotique", "Wadata Marché". Use distinct believable shopfront/food-market photographs. No repeated "Voir la boutique" buttons or extra promotional text.
5. At the bottom, a white mobile navigation bar with four evenly spaced outlined icons and labels: "Accueil", "Catalogue", "Panier", "Compte". Accueil is selected emerald; other items muted. Add a small "4" badge to Panier. All content ends above this bar with comfortable clearance.
STRICT exclusions: no hero banner, no carousel, no large promotional photograph above the categories, no "Que recherchez-vous ?" heading, no "Voir toutes les offres" button, no repeated service/product sections, no slogans, no ratings or invented verification claims, no tiny unreadable copy, no floating elements obscuring cards. The page should feel immediately understandable and visually close to the user's existing BabaMarket screenshots, simply cleaner and shorter. Exact French accents and spelling. Deliver only the UI image.
